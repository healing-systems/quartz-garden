Ancestors can provide practical business guidance through dream work—not just emotional or spiritual insight, but actual decision-making clarity. This challenges the separation between "spiritual practice" and "practical life."

**Process:**

1. Before sleep, ask ancestors a specific question about business decisions, creative direction, or strategic choices
2. Upon waking, capture whatever remains of the dream, even if incomplete
3. If dreams slip away too quickly to remember, pull three tarot cards and use those as the messages from the dreams that couldn't be captured

The practice tracks themes over time rather than seeking single-answer revelations. Ancestral wisdom through dreams often arrives as clarity that feels "obvious" in hindsight—like "we've just been waiting for you to ASK." This suggests the wisdom is already accessible; dream work is about slowing down enough to receive it.

**Current application:** Using this practice for creative.intuitive business decisions. Ancestors are "beautifully loud right now" through dreams, providing guidance that feels more reliable than external consultation.
